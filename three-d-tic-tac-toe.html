Rules

The board is a 4 x 4 x 4 cube, shown as four stacked layers. × and ○ alternate placing one mark.

Win by making four in a straight line anywhere in the cube: on one layer, straight through the layers, or along a 3D diagonal.

If all 64 cubes fill with no four-in-a-row, the game is a draw.
