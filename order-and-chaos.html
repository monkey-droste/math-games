Rules

Order and Chaos is played on a 6 x 6 board. On every turn, the player may place either X or O.

Order wins as soon as there are five matching marks in a row horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.

Chaos wins if the whole board fills before Order makes a five-in-a-row.
