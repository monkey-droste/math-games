Rules

Each small board is a normal game of tic-tac-toe. The square you choose sends the next player to the matching small board.

If that target board is already won or full, the next player may move on any open board.

Variants

  1. Classic. Win small boards to claim big-board squares. Three claimed big-board squares in a row wins.
  2. Single Win. The first player to win any small board wins the whole game immediately.
  3. Majority Rule. Big-board rows do not matter. Win more small boards than your opponent.
  4. Ultimate Drop 3. Play like Ultimate Tic-Tac-Toe, except marks drop to the lowest open square in the chosen column of the small board.
  5. The Dual Game. Reverse the usual steering. The previous move's small board tells you which square position to fill, while you choose the small board.
  6. Shared Territory. Drawn small boards count for both players when checking big-board rows.
