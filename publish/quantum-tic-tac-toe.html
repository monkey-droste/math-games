Rules

  1. Each turn places two linked copies of the current mark in two different open squares.
  2. The little number on a mark tells which turn created that quantum pair.
  3. If the links form a loop, the newest pair collapses: choose one end, and the forced chain makes real Xs and Os.
  4. After collapse, ordinary tic-tac-toe lines decide the winner. If both players finish lines, the earlier completed line wins.
