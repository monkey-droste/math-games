White starts. Choose an amazon.

Move like a queen, then shoot an arrow like a queen.

Mode
Your Side

Rules

On your turn, move one amazon in any straight or diagonal line, like a chess queen.

From the amazon's new square, shoot one arrow in any straight or diagonal line. The arrow blocks that square forever.

If you begin your turn with no legal move, you lose.

Source

This unofficial fan-made page is inspired by Ben Orlin's Math Games with Bad Drawings.

Variants

6 x 6 is a tiny two-amazon duel. 8 x 8 is a fast learning board. 10 x 10 is the classic setup.
