Gold starts. Choose an amazon.

Move like a queen, then shoot an arrow like a queen.

Rules

Gold moves first, then Teal. Each side controls the amazons in its own board color.

On your turn, move one amazon in any straight or diagonal line, like a chess queen.

From the amazon's new square, shoot one arrow in any straight or diagonal line. That square disappears from the board.

If you begin your turn with no legal move, you lose.

Variants

6 x 6 is a tiny two-amazon duel. 8 x 8 is a fast three-amazon board. 10 x 10 is the classic setup.
